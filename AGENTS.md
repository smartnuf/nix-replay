# Repository instructions

## Purpose

This repository develops a podcast and companion publication about Nix,
NixOS, and replayable human-machine software development.

Read README.md and the documents under docs/ before changing project
direction.

## Current authority

Discovery gate D0 is approved. The project is in research design D1.

Work may specify research questions, source strategy, historical method,
practical experiments, claim taxonomy, and treatment of disputed matters.

Do not begin the evidence dossier, generate podcast scripts or audio, create
feeds or releases, or distribute material until the applicable review gate in
docs/process.md is explicitly approved.

## Working rules

- Preserve the distinction between fact, report, inference, hypothesis, and
  project judgement.
- Prefer primary and authoritative sources for research.
- Verify current claims rather than relying on model memory.
- Treat claims that Nix lacks a capability as research questions until the
  relevant Nix and surrounding ecosystem have been examined.
- Do not use external photographs, audio, or artwork without recording origin,
  licence, attribution, and intended use.
- Keep prose and Markdown lines at no more than 79 characters where practical.
- Keep changes focused and expose consequential choices rather than making
  silent product or editorial decisions.
- Use durable repository records for accepted intent, evidence, decisions, and
  results. Do not treat a conversation transcript as authoritative state.

## Human-reserved decisions

Explicit owner approval is required for:

- the vision and significant changes to it;
- the public-facing title and primary audience;
- the series and production specifications;
- final scripts and generated voices;
- licences and third-party rights decisions;
- publishing or distributing audio and companion material; and
- changing the review or generation boundaries.
