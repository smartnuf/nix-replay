# Provisional editorial plan

Status: hypothesis for research and review, not an approved episode list

## Narrative movement

The likely narrative begins with concrete systems problems, reconstructs the
history and architecture of Nix, tests those ideas in practice, and then asks
what they imply for a broader replayable development process.

## Possible parts

### 1. Origins and people

What problems motivated Nix? Which research traditions and earlier systems
influenced it? Who developed Nix and NixOS, and how did institutions and the
community shape their evolution?

### 2. The architectural wager

Investigate declarative descriptions, purity, immutable artefacts, dependency
closure, reproducibility, composition, rollback, and the relationships among
them.

### 3. Specifications that do work

Ask whether descriptions can actively govern repeatable yet adjustable
software and systems processes rather than merely document them.

### 4. Environments that can move

Test reproducible, controllable, and relocatable environments for building,
testing, debugging, deployment, and investigation.

### 5. Where reality leaks through

Study hardware, mutable data, secrets, external services, interactive work,
platform differences, partial failure, ownership, authority, and other
boundaries that cannot simply be made pure.

### 6. From reproducible artefacts to replayable development

Ask what is needed to govern a process that unfolds over time, permits bounded
choice and novelty, learns from evaluation, and can branch from checkpoints
under different steering.

### 7. Lessons worth transferring

Identify what should influence cross-platform tooling, development-process
design, human-machine orchestration, and systems architecture. Separate
transferable results from features peculiar to Nix.

## Companion-material pattern

Every approved episode should eventually have:

- one stable canonical page;
- a synopsis and chapter markers;
- a reviewed transcript;
- a claim and source register;
- links and further reading;
- original or lawfully reusable images;
- diagrams and timelines;
- practical examples or experiments where relevant;
- corrections and version history; and
- an obvious link from podcast-app show notes.

The publishing mechanism and metadata standards will be researched and
specified before production.
