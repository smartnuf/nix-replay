# Development and review process

The project should use the discipline it is investigating. Its repository is
the durable state; conversations are working surfaces.

The aim is useful control with little ceremonial or adjudication burden.
Records should concentrate on intent, evidence, decision, action, and result.

## Stages and gates

### D0: Intent discovery

Capture the purpose, definitions, audience, scope, desired listener outcome,
and important non-goals.

Exit requires explicit approval of the vision.

### D1: Research design

Specify the questions, source strategy, historical method, practical
experiments, claim taxonomy, and treatment of disputed matters.

Exit requires review of the research plan.

### D2: Evidence dossier

Collect and assess sources, chronology, people, concepts, ecosystem evidence,
experiments, contradictions, and uncertainties.

Exit requires an evidence review sufficient to outline the series.

### D3: Series specification

Approve the narrative, number and purpose of episodes, companion-material
model, tone, voices, approximate duration, and acceptance criteria.

Exit requires explicit approval of the series specification.

### D4: Episode specification and script

For each episode, approve its question, listener outcome, claims, sources,
outline, visual plan, and then its complete script.

No audio is generated from an unapproved script.

### D5: Production trial

Generate a bounded sample, evaluate voices, pacing, pronunciation, accessibility,
audio quality, metadata, and the relationship with companion material.

Exit requires explicit approval of the production specification.

### D6: Production and publication

Generate and quality-check the approved artefacts. Publication, distribution,
licensing, and consequential external communication remain separately
authorised actions.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer primary and authoritative sources.
- Record the source and date for factual claims.
- Mark report, inference, hypothesis, and project judgement distinctly.
- Preserve material disagreement and uncertainty.
- Do not infer absence merely because evidence has not yet been found.
- Verify time-sensitive claims close to scripting and again before release.
- Track the origin, licence, attribution, and permitted use of external media.
- Treat generated images as illustrative unless separately evidenced.

## Change and review rules

- Main represents accepted project state.
- Conduct substantive changes on focused branches and pull requests.
- Use issues for bounded research questions and decisions.
- Use ADRs for durable choices that constrain later work.
- Keep unresolved questions explicit rather than silently choosing defaults.
- Review the exact current head and resolve consequential findings before
  merging.
- A later stage must not silently invalidate an earlier approved decision.
- Reopen the relevant gate when evidence changes the premise of an approval.

## Generation boundary

Until D4 is approved, do not generate podcast audio.

Exploratory diagrams, research notes, and non-release mock-ups may be created
when they help the review, but must be labelled clearly. Do not publish or
distribute any episode merely because production artefacts exist.
