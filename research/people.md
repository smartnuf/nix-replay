## Editorial safeguards

- Say "the paper credits" when the evidence is a byline.
- Do not equate the earliest paper found with sole invention.
- Do not infer a person's present role from an old publication.
- Seek consent and current self-description before portraying living people in
  an episode or using interview material.
- Expand this map through repository history, governance records, archived
  discussions, and interviews; do not let academic publication alone define
  whose work counts.
